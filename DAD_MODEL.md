# DAD — Dollar a Day Structural Model

Status: Working model / good for now.

This document records the current DAD structure, payment flow, payout logic, implementation path, and guardrails.

DAD stands for Dollar a Day.

Core sentence:

> One dollar a day. One community at a time.

DAD is a contribution-facing companion to IPM.db.

DAD raises and routes predictable community funding.

IPM.db records contributions, allocations, outcomes, agreements, and public accountability.

AJF&Co. develops, operates, promotes, and improves the system.

## 2. Public structure

DAD should be explained in plain public language:

> DAD — Dollar a Day is a simple contribution stream for housing action and local community outcomes.

> Contributions are tracked through IPM.db so the public can see money coming in, money going out, and outcomes attached to each allocation.

Public contribution language must remain careful until charitable status or a qualified donee / charity partner is confirmed.

Do not promise tax receipts unless donations are processed through an eligible charity or qualified donee.

## 4. Gross revenue structure

Simple default revenue formula:

For every $1.00 received:

- $0.10 = AJF&Co. operating/development share
- $0.10 = direct promotion / sales commission pool
- $0.01 = secondary-level referral commission pool
- $0.79 = DAD program allocation pool

This is the first proposed model and requires legal/accounting review before being represented as final.

Plain-language statement:

> Ten cents funds AJF&Co. system operation and development. Ten cents rewards direct growth. One cent supports secondary referral growth. The remainder funds DAD community allocations and public outcomes.

---

## 5. Commission model

The commission model must be simple, capped, and based on real contribution revenue.

### Direct promoter commission

Direct promoter receives 10% of incoming contribution revenue they directly generate.

Example:

- Contributor gives $7/week.
- Direct promoter commission = $0.70.
- DAD records the attribution.

### Secondary referral commission

Secondary-level commission is 1% of incoming contribution revenue from a verified secondary relationship.

Example:

- Contributor gives $7/week.
- Secondary commission = $0.07.

### Commission guardrail

Commissions must be paid only from actual received contribution revenue.

No commission is paid for recruiting a person alone.

No commission is paid unless real contributions are received.

No payout should depend primarily on recruitment rather than contribution activity.

## 11. Compliance guardrails

DAD must avoid pyramid-style language and structure.

The model must be framed as contribution growth and service implementation, not recruitment for recruitment's sake.

The model must not promise charitable tax receipts until a qualified structure is in place.

The model must not imply that all contributions go directly to service delivery when AJF&Co. and promoter shares are being retained.

The model must disclose the allocation formula plainly.

Suggested disclosure:

> DAD uses a transparent allocation model. A defined share supports system operation, public growth, and tracked community service delivery. Allocation details are recorded through IPM.db.
