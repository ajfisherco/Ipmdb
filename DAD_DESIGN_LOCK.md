# DAD — Homepage Design Lock

Status: GFN / locked as current design direction.

This records the accepted visual direction for the DAD homepage and payment-facing public site.

## Core visual direction

DAD should feel:

- bright
- warm
- readable
- civic
- trustworthy
- inviting
- community-first
- simple enough to understand immediately

The design should not feel like a dense nonprofit report, a generic SaaS dashboard, or a technical IPM.db page.

## Primary public sentence

> One dollar a day. One community at a time.

This remains the main headline.

Use large, readable type.

On mobile, readability wins over trying to fit everything above the fold.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Logo / DAD identity
2. One large headline
3. One short support sentence
4. One large contribution button
5. Contribution equivalents
6. How it works
7. Transparency / IPM.db accountability note

## Main button rule

Use one dominant contribution button only.

Preferred wording:

> Contribute Now

Alternate wording:

> Contribute $1 a Day

The button should point to the Square payment page.

Do not create multiple competing payment buttons on the homepage.

Other items may explain the model, but the main action must be obvious.

## Contribution equivalents

Display the simple math clearly:

- Daily: $1
- Weekly: $7
- Monthly: $30
- Yearly: $365

These should be large, readable cards.

They are explanatory unless specific Square links are created for each option.

Current payment reality:

- Square supports the tested weekly subscription path.
- DAD is currently collected as $7/week through Square.

## How it works

Use three steps only:

1. Contribute
2. Allocate
3. Track

Plain explanations:

### Contribute

People give a small recurring contribution.

### Allocate

Funds are directed to housing, support services, and local community outcomes.

### Track

Every contribution, allocation, payout, and outcome is recorded through IPM.db.

## Hamburger menu

On mobile, the hamburger opens navigation.

Menu items:

- Home
- How It Works
- Services
- Public Ledger
- Partners
- About
- Contact

Desktop may show nav items directly, but hamburger may remain as an extra menu control if useful.

## Header

Header is good for now.

Keep:

- DAD — Dollar a Day
- clean nav
- simple menu / hamburger
- strong but not cluttered identity

Upgrade the logo design over time, but keep the basic symbol family:

- people/community
- heart
- shelter/home
- growth/support
- green/orange/yellow palette

## Desktop homepage direction

Locked desktop direction:

- wide hero layout
- headline on left or central-left
- warm community/housing image on right
- one large orange contribution button
- contribution cards below the hero
- strong dark-green “How It Works” band or section
- transparency/IPM.db accountability strip near the lower section

Desktop may include navigation links in the header:

- How It Works
- Services
- Public Ledger
- Partners
- About
- Contact

## Mobile homepage direction

Locked mobile direction:

- large readable header
- large mobile-first headline
- bright community illustration
- one huge orange contribution button
- large contribution equivalent cards in a 2x2 layout
- simple “How it works” section with large icons
- minimal small text

Mobile rule:

> Small words, big inviting buttons.

## Colours

Primary colours:

- deep green
- bright orange
- golden yellow
- warm cream
- soft sky blue
- fresh community greens

Colour should be livelier than the first prototype.

Avoid dull, overly pale, or washed-out sections.

## Typography

Use large readable type.

Hero headline should be very large.

Body copy should be minimal and readable on phone without zooming.

Small text should be avoided unless legally necessary.

## Content density rule

The page must not be cluttered.

Remove excess text.

Do not overload the first screen with explanations, fine print, or too many links.

Keep detailed explanations on secondary pages.

## Current accepted mockup qualities

The accepted direction includes:

- bold DAD logo in top-left
- large DAD — Dollar a Day header
- clear hamburger menu
- big headline
- orange emphasis on the word “community”
- warm housing/community illustration
- large orange contribution button
- 2x2 contribution cards on mobile
- clear three-step explanation
- green/orange/yellow palette
- cheerful civic tone

## Public positioning

DAD should be presented as:

> A simple contribution stream for housing action and local outcomes, tracked publicly through IPM.db.

Avoid overexplaining the commission structure on the homepage.

The homepage should sell trust, simplicity, and action.

Detailed allocation and promoter terms belong on dedicated pages.

## Good for now

This file locks the current visual and structural direction as the working design standard for DAD.
