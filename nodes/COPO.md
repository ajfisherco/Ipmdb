# COPO — Court of Public Opinion

COPO is a civic accountability node for IPM.db.

Asset ID: `IPMDB-0007`

It turns public-interest questions into a clean record.

## Core line

Do no harm. Tell the truth. Show the proof. Record the response.

## Compressed language

When trust breaks, the public record begins.

Say it. Prove it. Ask for response. Record what happens.

## Purpose

COPO helps organize questions, claims, source material, replies, corrections, and outcomes.

It is built for public trust review, not gossip.

## I2A flow

Issue → Claim → Evidence → Response Request → Public Record → Outcome Tracked

## Case checklist

Every COPO case should identify:

- the issue
- the claim
- the source
- the evidence
- what is known
- what is uncertain
- who should be asked to respond
- what response was requested
- what response was received
- what correction or outcome followed

## Operating rules

- Separate fact, claim, opinion, evidence, and conclusion.
- Preserve original wording where useful.
- Mark uncertainty clearly.
- Give a fair chance to respond where appropriate.
- Do not publish private personal information unless there is a clear public-interest reason and lawful basis.
- Do not make claims stronger than the evidence supports.
- Do not confuse anger with proof.

## Relationship to IPM.db

COPO is a node inside IPM.db.

IPM.db records the flow.

COPO applies that flow to public accountability.
