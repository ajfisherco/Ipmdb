# Fabrication & Prototyping

## Asset ID

IPMDB-0009

## Purpose

To bridge ideas, designs, and digital mockups into physical prototypes, fabrication patterns, moulds, and implementation-ready assets.

## Current Status

Draft node.

## Scope

- digital mockups
- CAD concepts
- STL and 3D-printing files
- pattern making
- mould development
- CNC concepts
- rapid prototyping
- material testing
- manufacturing coordination
- physical asset development

## Description

Fabrication & Prototyping is the practical bridge between development and manufacturing.

It supports the IPM.db process by helping ideas move from written concept to designed object, prototype, test article, mould, tool, or production pathway.

## Workflow Concept

1. Idea is submitted
2. Identity and attribution are recorded
3. Node assignment is made
4. Digital mockup is created
5. Prototype or pattern is prepared
6. Fabrication method is selected
7. Testing and revision occur
8. Resulting asset is recorded and linked back to contributors
