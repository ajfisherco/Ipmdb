One person at a time.

DAD starts with a small, repeatable contribution. The model is simple: gather support, direct it toward practical housing pathways, record what happened, and improve the system in public.

Built through AJF&Co. Tracked through IPM.db.

DAD is the contribution face. IPM.db is the record layer. AJF&Co. keeps the system moving from idea to public action.

Visit AJF&Co.

DAD is a companion initiative to IPM.db. We are endeavouring to become eligible for receiptable giving.

Tax receipts are not currently promised. Receipts require processing through an eligible charity or qualified donee, or DAD becoming eligible in its own right.
