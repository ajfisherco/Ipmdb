Purpose

DADS organizes simple daily contribution, local service, public records, and practical housing outcomes.

DADS moves support toward people and housing pathways one person at a time, one community at a time.

Membership

Members and helpers support outreach, local coordination, partner discovery, public records, media, events, practical services, and community delivery.

Members begin with minimal information and build their personal profile. Public-facing records may use anonymized or limited account information wherever practical.

Structure (proposed)

DADS operates with a charitable purpose, a board of directors, and AJF&Co. as the principal governance oversight body.

AJF&Co. remains the build space. IPM.db remains the public record layer. DADS serves as the participation and delivery structure.

Every contribution is recorded. Receipts are part of the operating flow.
