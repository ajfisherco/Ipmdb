Purpose

DADS exists to organize simple daily contribution, local service, public records, and practical housing outcomes.

The target is to help move support toward people and housing pathways one person at a time, one community at a time.

Membership

Members and helpers can support outreach, local coordination, partner discovery, public records, media, events, practical services, and community delivery.

Members can begin with minimal information and build their personal profile only as they see fit. Public-facing records may use anonymized or limited account information wherever practical.

Structure (proposed)

DAD is intended to operate with a charitable purpose and a board of directors over which Alexander John Fisher will preside.

The working governance model is consensus-seeking: the board deliberates, tests, and carries the 49% decision field, while the founder-presiding role holds final affirmative sign-off at the end of each instance.

AJF&Co. remains the build space. IPM.db remains the public record layer. DAD Society becomes the participation and delivery structure as the model is formalized.

Every contribution will be recorded. Receipts are part of the intended operating flow.
